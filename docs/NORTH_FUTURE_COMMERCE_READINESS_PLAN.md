# NORTH Future Commerce-Readiness Plan

**Phase:** 2G — planning only
**Status:** All commerce gates remain closed. This document is not a merchant brief, payment activation plan, or launch authorization.

> **Hard boundary:** No checkout, Stripe, Gumroad, Shopify, payment processor, merchant product, public price, delivery link, download, email capture, affiliate link, customer account, analytics/tracking, or deployment has been configured in Phase 2G.

## Readiness Matrix

| Readiness layer | What must be decided and proven | Current Phase 2G state | Owner before activation |
|---|---|---|---|

| Product package | Final approved buyer files, versioning, formats, inclusion/exclusion list, source/license decision | Private candidate only | Founder + product owner |
| Buyer proof | Truthful product page, real samples, copy/claim approval, digital-versus-physical disclosure | Private Phase 2G review page only | Founder + product owner |
| Pricing | Final price, currency, promotions, displayed taxes/fees, price-change policy | $19 is founder-only proposed candidate, not public | Founder + legal/operations |
| Payment processor | Processor selection, account verification, payment methods, dispute/refund capability, security configuration | Not selected or connected | Founder + finance/operations |
| Tax | Nexus review, tax collection/remittance responsibility, receipts, exemptions, international treatment | Not reviewed or configured | Qualified tax/legal adviser + operations |
| Privacy / customer data | Minimum data design, legal notices, retention, consent, breach/incident procedure, data processor review | No customer-data capture exists | Legal + privacy owner |
| Refund / support | Published policy, eligibility, response expectation, access-failure process, escalation path | Drafts only | Legal + support owner |
| Delivery / re-delivery | Secure buyer access, file versioning, expiration/re-delivery rules, lost-access support, auditability | Not configured | Product operations |
| Transactional communications | Receipt, confirmation, access, refund, and support messages; sender identity; deliverability tests | Not configured | Operations + support |

| End-to-end test | Controlled non-public order, payment confirmation, receipt, delivery, access recovery, refund/support simulation | Not possible until prior gates are approved | Operations + founder |

## Flagship Planner Release Sequence — Future Only

1. Founder approves the final English buyer-visible product package, proof page, visuals, price decision, and statement of what the purchaser receives.
2. Qualified legal review approves the license, support/refund wording, privacy, seller identity, and consumer disclosures.
3. Accessibility review verifies final purchaser files. No statement that files are fillable or accessible may be used unless those capabilities are actually tested and delivered.
4. The founder selects a merchant of record and payment approach after the above work is approved. No configuration occurs before explicit authorization.
5. Operations configures a secure private delivery and re-delivery path, exact receipts/confirmations, support inbox, and incident handling.
6. A controlled end-to-end test verifies payment, receipt, download/access, recovery, refund scenario, and customer-data controls.
7. Founder gives separate written approval for a limited commerce activation. Public deployment remains a separate decision.

## Explicit Non-Readiness

The Planner cannot be described as available, deliverable, “coming soon” for purchase, or ready to download. It is a private founder-review candidate only. No operational plan in this document grants permission to bypass legal, accessibility, tax, merchant, delivery, support, or founder gates.
