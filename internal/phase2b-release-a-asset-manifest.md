## Asset-state rules

The review exports use a typography-led NORTH treatment with deep navy, off-white, warm gold, muted blue, and system/browser fonts. They are intentionally not represented as final covers, final product photography, customer PDFs, fillable forms, or delivery assets. If a future release needs a cover, illustration, photography, icon set, licensed typeface, app screenshot, audio, or other media, it must be added to this manifest with its source, owner, license or release basis, locale status, alt-text/caption requirements, and founder approval status.

## Asset restrictions retained in Phase 2B

No third-party content has been downloaded, copied, or represented as rights-cleared. No customer-submitted content, testimonials, before/after material, personal data, or AI-generated visual identity asset is present. This keeps the present review package focused on original text and original layout code while the founder decides whether any future visual production is necessary.
