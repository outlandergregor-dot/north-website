# NORTH 1-3-5 Execution Planner — Support and Refund Information Draft v1.0

> **Founder review draft — not for sale or publication.**  
> **Working policy draft:** This document is not an active offer, does not establish a refund right, and requires qualified legal, consumer-rights, merchant, tax, and support-process review before activation.

## Purpose

This draft defines the future customer-information standard for a paid digital Planner Package. It is written to prevent misleading delivery claims, unclear refund expectations, or support promises that the operating team cannot meet. It must be reconciled with the final merchant platform’s buyer-protection rules and the consumer laws applicable to each approved selling location before release.

## Future support scope

For a future approved English release, NORTH support may help a verified purchaser with a missing order confirmation, broken download link, duplicate charge investigation, file-access problem, or a clear question about the listed file formats. Support is not coaching, project management, technical consulting, mental-health support, medical advice, legal advice, financial advice, or a guarantee that the planner will solve a personal or professional problem.

The final product page must state one monitored support address, the support operating hours or service expectation, the identity-verification method used before discussing an order, and the languages in which support is actually available. Until then, no customer-facing support commitment exists.

## Proposed future refund approach for founder decision

The recommended pilot posture is a **14-calendar-day satisfaction request window** from the confirmed purchase timestamp for the original purchaser. A purchaser would submit the request through the approved support method with the order email and order reference. The operating team would review the request against the final merchant platform’s rules, applicable mandatory consumer rights, fraud controls, and any previously issued refund.

A future approved refund policy should state plainly that a digital file may be delivered immediately after purchase, that consumer rights can vary by location, and that any legal right that cannot be waived takes priority over the commercial policy. It should not imply that every request is automatically approved, nor should it use language that conflicts with mandatory law or merchant policies.

## Delivery and access boundaries

The future customer purchase flow must disclose whether files are delivered on a confirmation page, by signed-in account, through a merchant download link, by transactional email, or by another approved method. It must also disclose download limits, link expiry behavior, re-delivery process, accessibility format availability, and whether updates are included.

This Phase 2D pilot does not activate a checkout, order confirmation, customer account, email, download link, delivery system, or refund processing. No person has a right to request an order remedy from this draft.

## Product-quality escalation

If a purchaser reports a materially corrupted PDF, inaccessible file, missing listed component, or a file that does not match the approved product description, the future support process should preserve the report, verify the purchased version, test the file, and determine whether replacement, re-delivery, correction, refund, or another lawful remedy applies. The detailed procedure remains a pre-launch operating dependency.

## Required activation checklist

Before this policy can be customer-facing, the founder must approve the price and file bundle, an attorney must review the customer language and jurisdictional requirements, the chosen merchant platform must be configured and tested, the support inbox and triage ownership must be live, the fulfillment process must be verified, the privacy and transactional-email disclosures must be approved, and the product’s language-readiness matrix must match the languages actually sold and supported.

## Activation condition

This draft is only a private operating proposal. It does not create a purchase, refund, cancellation, support, or delivery promise. It remains inactive until an explicit founder approval opens the future commerce pilot.
