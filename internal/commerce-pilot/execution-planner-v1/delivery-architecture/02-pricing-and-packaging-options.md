> **Founder decision record — not a public price list.** These options are private discussion inputs only. No price has been displayed publicly, configured in a merchant system, connected to payment, or offered for purchase.

## Recommended pilot decision

The recommended initial pilot is the **Core Digital Planner at $19 USD** after final approval. This option is the cleanest test of whether the 14-day planning system, premium visual direction, and private delivery experience create enough value on their own. It avoids expanding the first paid offer into editable-source support, format complexity, or a launch-discount promise before the delivery and support process is tested.

| Private option | Proposed price | Future included files | Advantages | Approval and operating conditions |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Core Digital Planner | **$19 USD** | Final English US Letter PDF, final English A4 PDF, start guide, fictional worked example | Clear offer; lowest initial support and licensing complexity | Approve final files, merchant, payment/tax/refund configuration, delivery, support, legal documents, and English only |
| Planner + Editable Source | **$29 USD** | Core files plus an approved editable version in one defined format | Higher perceived utility for advanced users | Decide allowed editable format, protect source rights, define re-distribution restriction, test support burden, confirm accessibility and delivery implications |
| Controlled launch offer | **$14 USD** | Core Digital Planner only | Can test initial message and purchase behavior at lower price | Approve campaign duration, price comparison rules, merchant display, refund configuration, support capacity, and no misleading urgency language |

## Recommendation logic

The $19 core option keeps the initial customer promise specific: a self-guided English two-week planning tool in approved printable formats. The editable-source option should remain a later decision because it changes licensing, support expectations, version control, and potential redistribution risk. The launch option should not be used merely to create urgency; it should be chosen only after the founder decides that a controlled pilot needs a clear, time-bounded test condition.

## Pricing is not readiness

A price choice does not open a product for sale. Before any price is visible, the product must satisfy content, visual, accessibility, policy, merchant, delivery, support, privacy, refund, legal, tax, language, and founder approval gates. The price must match the actual bundle, not a future promise.
