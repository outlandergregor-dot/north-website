## Product-page copy candidate

### Outcome

**Two weeks. One clear direction.** The NORTH 1-3-5 Execution Planner is a calm two-week PDF planning rhythm for choosing one meaningful priority, three supporting actions, and five useful small wins—without turning your day into a verdict.

### What is included

The proposed Core Digital Edition includes the English planner in both US Letter and A4 print formats. Each edition contains Start Here guidance, a two-week intention page, fourteen daily planning pages, two weekly reset/review pages, a continuation plan, and one clearly labelled fictional example.

### Who it is for

It is designed for adults who want a practical, repeatable structure for focus, routine, and follow-through, especially after trying NORTH’s free 1-3-5 Daily Page.

### Who it is not for

It is not designed as medical or mental-health treatment, individualized professional advice, financial planning, project-management software, or a promise of a particular result.

### Format and print sizes

The proposed product is a digital PDF bundle only. It is not a physical book, hardcover, shipped kit, subscription, course, or editable-template license. The proposed bundle includes US Letter and A4 PDFs.

### Personal use

The intended license is a personal-use license only. The customer may print reasonable personal copies for their own use but may not redistribute, resell, sublicense, share publicly, or turn the Planner into a client service, template bundle, or group resource without a separate written license.

### Support and refund path

The support and refund language remains a **working draft pending legal and operations review**. Before any sale, NORTH must publish a truthful support contact, response expectation, refund window, eligible/inedible refund conditions, access-failure process, and applicable consumer-rights language through the chosen merchant and product page.
