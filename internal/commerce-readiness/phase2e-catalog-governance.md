# NORTH Phase 2E — Governed Product Catalog Roadmap

> **Roadmap status: private founder planning only.** The $19 English 1-3-5 Execution Planner Core Digital Edition remains the only proposed commercial pilot. This document does not create a product listing, price, checkout, delivery, affiliate link, or public launch.

## Product-architecture correction

NORTH serves four connected life pillars: **financial clarity and household stability; routines, discipline, and life organization; focus, execution, and recovery from overwhelm; and motivation, goals, decision-making, and personal direction.** The fifth catalog family—**Authority and Education**—is a cross-cutting system for teaching the NORTH Method; it is not a separate life pillar.

Every product must have a meaningful relationship to the NORTH Method: **Safe Number, Big Thing, 1-3-5 execution, weekly reflection, routines, recovery, and growth.** A product cannot enter production merely because it has a marketable name; it requires a useful purpose, bounded promise, rights/ownership evidence, production plan, safety and policy review, localization readiness, accessibility/print QA where relevant, and founder approval.

## Family briefs

### Financial Clarity

The Financial Clarity family makes household organization and calm conversations easier without telling a person what to do with money. The Safe Number Starter Kit, Family Money Meeting Kit, Paycheck-to-Priority Planner, and Financial Calm Workbook must remain general educational materials. They must not make individualized tax, investment, debt, legal, income, credit, or financial recommendations. Each requires legal/policy review and content-specialist review before public sale.

### Routine Reset

The Routine Reset family supports repeatable daily and weekly structures. The 21-Day Routine Reset, Morning and Evening Ritual Cards, Life Admin Reset, and Weekly Reset Kit should help users notice patterns, make one bounded change, and review evidence without promising transformation, medical treatment, or productivity results. The Weekly Reset Kit has the earliest post-pilot position because it must be demonstrably distinct from the Planner.

### Focus and Execution

The Focus and Execution family turns a meaningful priority into a manageable next action. It includes the 1-3-5 Execution Planner, Focus Recovery Protocol, Deep Work Sprint Kit, and Distraction Reset Toolkit. All materials must be non-clinical and avoid medical, mental-health, income, or guaranteed-performance claims.

### Direction and Motivation

The Direction and Motivation family connects values, decisions, goals, and action. The Decision Clarity Toolkit, 30-Day NORTH Journey, Momentum Journal, and Purpose and Goals Compass should help users reflect and choose bounded next steps. They must not promise personal transformation or substitute for legal, financial, medical, or professional advice.

### Authority and Education

The Authority and Education family converts NORTH’s original principles into long-form, age-appropriate, or audience-specific education. The NORTH Method e-book / audiobook must be first-party authored only; no copied, resold, PLR, or unlicensed material is permitted. Financial Foundations for Families carries the same educational-only financial boundaries. NORTH Launch Kit for ages 16–25 requires age, privacy, and safeguarding review. NORTH for Founders must not make business, revenue, financial, tax, legal, or investment claims.

## Unified availability rule

A locale route does not equal a product edition. English is the only private pilot candidate. Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish (Latin America) are first in the human-review queue only after full translation of product copy, PDFs, print proof, support/refund copy, policy/legal content, and founder approval. Arabic is an RTL-readiness route only and requires full RTL deliverable QA before any offer. Every other locale is not yet available for product content, checkout, delivery, support, or policy.

## Pre-release invariant

No future product can display a purchase action, price, checkout, preorder, waitlist, delivery, customer download, subscription, affiliate link, or language-specific availability until the canonical catalog record passes every required release dependency. The Phase 2E catalog registry is the source of truth for that control.
