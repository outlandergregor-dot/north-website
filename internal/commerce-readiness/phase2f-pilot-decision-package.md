# NORTH Phase 2F — Pre-Commerce Validation and Pilot Decision Package

> **Status: founder decision document only.** No merchant, payment, checkout, public price, customer delivery, customer account, email capture, analytics, affiliate, or deployment capability is configured.

## Proposed small paid-pilot plan

| Decision area | Founder-review proposal |
|---|---|
| Pilot product | 1-3-5 Execution Planner — Core Digital Edition v1.0 |
| Candidate customer | Adults who want a calm, printable two-week structure for one priority, three supports, five small wins, weekly reflection, and an achievable next step |
| Candidate price | $19 USD; English only; discussion input only, not public price copy |
| Candidate cohort | Start with a deliberately small founder-approved cohort after legal, merchant, delivery, and support validation; exact size must be selected by founder |
| Proposed bundle | US Letter PDF + A4 PDF + short start guidance; no editable source in the first Core Digital bundle |
| Product claim boundary | A self-guided organizational tool; no therapy, diagnosis, treatment, productivity guarantee, personalized financial, legal, tax, investment, debt, credit, income, or other professional advice |
| Success signals | Completed secure delivery, support/refund process working, low fulfillment errors, clear customer understanding of format and use, documented refund reasons, and no material policy/accessibility issue |
| Failure / rollback | Pause sales, stop public product visibility, preserve order/support/refund records, fulfill outstanding valid purchases, document root cause, and return to founder review before resuming |

## Future merchant and delivery decision checklist

NORTH must select a future provider only after an updated, neutral comparison of merchant-of-record/tax responsibility, eligible buyer countries, receipts, refund tooling, payout timing, secure delivery, verified events or webhooks, support burden, data export, privacy terms, cost structure, and affiliate readiness. The official-source research and comparison record from Phase 2E remains a research input rather than a provider selection.[1] [2] [3]

> **Future intended flow:** product page → merchant-hosted checkout → verified payment event → receipt → secure delivery → support/refund record. NORTH must never collect, transmit, or store payment-card data.

The following gates must all be closed before a real transaction: merchant eligibility verified; seller/tax/receipt responsibilities reviewed; final English product/price/license/refund/support/privacy/consumer wording approved; payment and refund simulations completed; delivery/re-delivery tested; inaccessible-file fallback documented; support owner and service timeframe set; receipt and record-retention logic tested; and founder activates exactly one product/edition in writing.

## Measurement and privacy controls

The future closed-beta measurement model is minimum-data only. It may measure product-page view, checkout start, completed purchase, delivery success, support contact, refund request, refund completion, and voluntary product feedback only after legal/privacy review and an approved merchant or privacy-conscious analytics configuration. It must not activate a tracking pixel, behavioral profiling, customer account, newsletter, automatic sharing, or sensitive NORTH-data collection in this phase.

## Founder decisions still required before any sale

1. Approve the final Core Digital product content, visual asset selection, and $19 price.
2. Obtain qualified legal review for license, refund/support, privacy, consumer-law, tax, financial-education, and jurisdiction-specific wording.
3. Select a merchant only after current terms, seller eligibility, global coverage, tax/receipt, refund, support, data, delivery, and payout requirements are verified.
4. Confirm the support owner, refund authority, operating timeframe, re-delivery process, inaccessible-file support, and data-retention practice.
5. Approve a non-public end-to-end test plan before any customer sees checkout.
6. Provide separate written authorization before activating any merchant, payment, checkout, public price, delivery, customer record, tracking, or launch.
