# Phase 2E Official Merchant Research Notes

> **Decision status: research only. No provider has been selected, connected, or configured.**

## Findings used in the neutral comparison

| Area | Official-source finding | Phase 2E implication |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant of record | Lemon Squeezy defines a merchant of record as the legal entity selling to the end customer and says it handles payments, sales tax, refunds, chargebacks, and PCI responsibility. | A future merchant-of-record candidate can reduce operational responsibility, but it does not remove the need for founder legal, policy, product, and support review. |
| Digital-goods tax | Shopify’s official help explains that digital goods may have jurisdiction-specific tax treatment and advises merchants to consult a local tax expert. Its EU example highlights VAT by customer location. | A direct-store candidate creates configuration and professional-review work; it cannot be presumed tax-ready worldwide. |
| Digital product reseller model | Gumroad’s terms state that suppliers may appoint Gumroad as a non-exclusive reseller, with Gumroad acting as merchant of record for resales conducted through its service. The terms describe delivery facilitation, first-tier post-sale support related to invoices/refunds/chargebacks, and indirect-tax collection/reporting/remittance services. | A reseller/MoR candidate should be evaluated against current eligibility, territory support, actual seller obligations, data exports, refund handling, brand control, and product-specific policy requirements at the time of launch. |

## Method constraint

The Phase 2E comparison must not treat any platform’s marketing claims, features, territories, fees, tax coverage, payment methods, refund process, affiliate capability, delivery behavior, or data-export capability as a launch guarantee. The founder must re-verify current terms, account eligibility, product eligibility, location coverage, policies, implementation behavior, and legal/tax consequences before provider selection or any customer sale.
