## Phase 2B entry condition

Phase 2B cannot start merely because a product has a concept page. At least one Release A offer must have a founder-approved complete original asset, final file QA, delivery/support/policy readiness, and a separately approved merchant/delivery plan. Money products require the qualified-review evidence listed above before any public sales preparation.

## Phase 2A exclusion confirmation

No dependency in this register was satisfied by configuration, customer data collection, checkout setup, Gumroad product creation, Namecheap/Vercel change, or public publishing during Phase 2A.
