## Page-level outline and original-content requirement

All pages require original founder-approved content. The outline is a production scaffold only.

1. **Cover and use note.** Explain the planner’s 14-day scope; do not promise transformation.
2. **How 1-3-5 works.** Concise method explanation, an example that is clearly illustrative, and a reminder to adapt to reality.
3. **Day 1–14 planning spreads.** One Big Thing, three supporting actions, five quick wins, a realistic boundary, and a short end-of-day note.
4. **Day 7 checkpoint.** Notice what is working, simplify the second week, and decide what to leave out.
5. **Day 14 reflection.** Review completed evidence, friction, and one next practice—without scorekeeping or guilt framing.
6. **Continuation page.** Point to the free Daily Page and optional NORTH app without creating an entitlement requirement.
