## Product promise

The program should guide an adult through seven brief, practical days of clearing one priority, reducing avoidable friction, and establishing a realistic daily focus rhythm. It must be a complete standalone program without requiring the NORTH app.

| Field | Working specification |
|---|---|
| Intended buyer | Adults who want a short, bounded reset around priorities and attention. |
| Not for | Burnout treatment, clinical support, therapy, crisis care, diagnosis, or performance guarantees. |
| Proposed internal price | $27 USD; internal planning only, never display in Phase 2A. |
| Final formats required | Seven short lessons/actions, seven mission sheets, a reset workbook, weekly reflection, and completion plan in final accessible digital formats. |
| Product-to-app relationship | Optional daily implementation support after completion; no lesson may depend on app access. |
| Support expectation | Clear scope language, support owner, escalation wording, and final policy before sale. |

## Lesson-level outline and original-content requirement

All lesson language must be original, practical, brief, and non-clinical. It may not diagnose attention conditions, promise recovery, advise users to replace healthcare, or imply that focus difficulties have a single remedy.

| Day | Working lesson theme | Required original output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name the one direction | Short lesson and one decision worksheet. |
| 2 | Clear the immediate field | Friction inventory and one practical simplification action. |
| 3 | Protect a small block | Boundary-setting exercise without productivity promise. |
| 4 | Make the day smaller | 1-3-5-based mission sheet. |
| 5 | Review what is real | Evidence, friction, and adjustment reflection. |
| 6 | Choose a sustainable rhythm | A modest personal rhythm experiment. |
| 7 | Close the reset | Weekly reflection and realistic continuation plan. |

## Future sales-page and FAQ brief

The future page should state the seven-day time commitment, exact deliverables, standalone use, scope limitations, support route, and optional app relationship. It must avoid “burnout recovery,” “heal your attention,” “transform your life,” outcome guarantees, urgency, clinical language, testimonials, and fake social proof.

The final FAQ should answer: Who is it for? Who is it not for? Is this therapy or treatment? How much time per day? What files are included? Does it require the app? How is support handled? How do refund terms work? Where should someone seek urgent or clinical help? The final answer must direct urgent situations to appropriate qualified local services, not NORTH.
