## Product promise

The toolkit should help an adult put one meaningful decision into a calmer, documented process: identify what matters, make trade-offs visible, run a pre-mortem, and write a concise Captain’s Brief. It must work without the NORTH app and must never substitute for legal, medical, financial, tax, investment, or other professional advice.

| Field | Working specification |
|---|---|
| Intended buyer | Adults facing a meaningful personal or professional decision who want a structured reflection process. |

## Page-level outline and original-content requirement

1. **Start with the decision.** State the decision in one plain sentence, name the decision date if relevant, and avoid artificial urgency.
2. **Decision Filter.** Define what must be true, what matters most, and what belongs outside the decision.
3. **Trade-off Map.** Name trade-offs honestly rather than searching for a frictionless answer.
4. **Pre-mortem.** Explore plausible failure points as a reflection exercise, not a risk prediction or professional review.
5. **Captain’s Brief.** Summarize the current choice, open questions, next action, and review date.
6. **Close and continue.** Offer a practical free tool or optional app habit; never suggest that the product requires the app.

Every prompt, example, and instruction must be original and approved by the founder. Do not include copied frameworks, third-party diagrams, generic AI prompt bundles, legal/financial scenarios, or claims that the toolkit produces better decisions.
