## Product-specific founder questions

### 1-3-5 Execution Planner

Does the two-week intention make the daily 1–3–5 pattern feel more coherent without turning it into a rigid productivity system? Are the fourteen daily prompts distinct enough to avoid repetition? Is the fictional “Ren’s portfolio refresh” example helpful and believable? The founder should identify any daily page that needs shorter fields, larger writing space, different order, or a more direct completion check.

### Decision Clarity Toolkit

Does the process distinguish an ordinary self-guided decision from one requiring qualified help clearly enough? Do the Decision Brief, Filter, Trade-off Map, Pre-mortem, Captain’s Brief, and Decision Log produce a useful record without telling the user what to choose? The founder should review the fictional examples and confirm that they remain non-regulated and non-advisory.

### Focus Recovery Program

Does each day have a specific operational output rather than generic encouragement? Does the program avoid therapy-like, diagnostic, or burnout-treatment language throughout? The founder should review whether the seven-day pace is realistic and whether the final continuation ritual has enough structure without creating a rigid routine.

Review one product at a time. Begin with the HTML review export at normal desktop width, then the 390px mobile view, then inspect the US Letter and A4 review PDFs for visual rhythm.
