## Support / FAQ draft

> **Internal wording draft. It makes no availability or delivery promise.**

**What is this designed to help with?** It offers a short, repeatable way to decide what matters today, make a realistic plan, and notice what helped or blocked progress over two weeks.

**Do I need the NORTH app?** No. This planner is designed to work independently. The app may later be useful as an optional daily companion, but it is not required.

**How long does it take?** The suggested daily setup is under ten minutes. The weekly and two-week reviews are intentionally short; users can stop after the questions that are useful.

**What if I miss a day?** Continue from the next available page. The draft does not use streaks or penalties; the review pages are there to notice friction and choose a smaller next step.

**Is this professional advice or a performance guarantee?** No. It is a self-guided planning structure, not legal, medical, financial, investment, tax, employment, or other professional advice. It does not guarantee productivity or any result.
