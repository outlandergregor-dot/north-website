# Focus Recovery Program — v0.1 Founder Review Brief

> **Founder review draft — not for sale**

## Working purpose

This private draft develops a seven-day workflow reset for an adult who wants to make a crowded field of obligations more manageable. The intended practical outputs are a reduced list, one named priority, one usable attention block, and a small continuation habit. It is designed to stand alone, with no NORTH app requirement.

This is not a clinical, medical, therapeutic, diagnostic, treatment, crisis, or burnout resource. It makes no claim about health, attention, mood, wellbeing, or productivity outcomes.
