## One-page product brief

| Field | Draft decision |
|---|---|
| Promise | Offer an adult a calm, documented process for turning a difficult everyday personal or work decision into clear options, visible trade-offs, one next move, and a short learning record. |
| Intended buyer | An adult facing a meaningful non-professional decision that feels vague, crowded, or repeatedly delayed. |
| Not for | Legal, medical, financial, investment, tax, insurance, debt, immigration, compliance, safety-critical, or other professional decisions. It is not expert advice, a risk guarantee, or a substitute for consulting a qualified professional. |
| Scope | A self-guided worksheet process: Decision Brief, Decision Filter, Options & Trade-off Map, Pre-mortem, Captain’s Brief, and Decision Log, including two fully fictional examples. |
| Time commitment | One 45–75 minute working session, plus a short review at the user-selected review date. Users may pause or use one worksheet at a time. |
| Product-to-app relationship | Fully useful without NORTH. The app may later support a reminder or record, but there is no app requirement, data connection, entitlement, or advice feature. |
| Proposed price later | $19 planning metadata only. It must not render in a product page, review export, or founder-review UI. |

## Support / FAQ draft

> **Internal wording draft. It makes no availability or delivery claim.**

**What kind of decision is this for?** It is for ordinary personal or work-process choices where a structured reflection and record may help. It is not for decisions that require qualified legal, medical, financial, investment, tax, insurance, debt, immigration, compliance, or other professional advice.

**Does the toolkit tell me what to choose?** No. It helps organize the decision, assumptions, options, trade-offs, and first next move. The user remains responsible for the decision and for seeking qualified help where appropriate.

**Why include a pre-mortem?** It offers a practical way to notice assumptions and early warnings before committing. It is not a prediction method or safety assessment.

**Do I need the NORTH app?** No. The toolkit is designed to work independently. Any future app connection would be optional and separately approved.
