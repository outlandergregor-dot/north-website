## Art direction

The NORTH visual direction is **editorial, calm, aspirational, practical, and trustworthy**. Use official midnight navy (#071A2D), ivory (#F7F3EB), and restrained gold (#C8A96B), with warm early-morning natural light, matte paper, soft linen or walnut desk surfaces, intentional negative space, and the supplied NORTH compass icon as the only logo reference. Avoid generic hustle culture, neon gradients, excessive glow, childish illustration, fake luxury, crowded productivity dashboards, recognizable people, rendered text, fake reviews, or impossible planner pages.

## Generation prompt standards

All generated lifestyle scenes must be silent, text-free, non-human or use only non-identifiable cropped hands if necessary; the safest default is no person. When a real planner page is visible, use a real exported page as the dominant document reference. Do not generate fake planner markings, handwriting, pages, product packaging, testimonials, analytics, guarantees, or commerce UI.

## Canva output plan

Canva outputs will package only approved source visuals and text-free visual compositions: planner-cover and mockup layout, hero composition, sequential product-detail montage, Instagram portrait, Story/Reel cover, and a private product-preview visual guide. All Canva materials remain founder-review candidates and will not use a Brand Kit without explicit founder selection.

## QA gates

1. Every page shown must be an actual Planner page or be labelled illustrative.
2. All visual surfaces must use the official NORTH compass icon unchanged.
3. No generated text needs to carry factual or legal meaning.
4. No visual may show a physical shipped product, fake customer, fake outcome, fake review, checkout, payment, download, app requirement, or financial claim.
5. Final web exports must be optimized and labelled with source, prompt/reference, dimensions, intended usage, and alt text in the asset inventory.
