# Find Your North — Concept Brief v0.1

> **Internal concept only. Not a completed book, preorder, paperback, audiobook, or approved product.**
> **Registry ID:** `north.release-c.find-your-north-book`
> **Current gate:** `concept`

## Intended outcome

Develop a real authored long-form introduction to the NORTH life operating system, beginning with a finished ebook and considering paperback and audiobook only after the manuscript, editing, rights, and production path are complete.

## Editorial standard

| Field | Required direction |
|---|---|
| Intended reader | A reader who prefers a durable long-form introduction to the NORTH method. |
| Not for | A low-content journal, an AI-generated filler artifact, a pre-order without a manuscript, or a promise of personal transformation. |
| Required deliverables | Authored manuscript, professional edit, rights-cleared cover/interior, final ebook format, and later print/audio feasibility review. |
| Founder ownership | Product promise, original manuscript, final brand voice, and final approval. |
| Manus role | Production brief, package structure, website preview architecture, QA, and later approved delivery architecture. |

## Development requirements

The founder must create or approve the original manuscript. Editorial development must include a substantive review, copy edit, proofread, rights review for all text and imagery, professional cover/interior design, accessible ebook production, and a final distribution/support plan. Audio requires original or properly licensed narration and separate rights, production, localization, and wellness-claim review.

## Honest public state

The Phase 2A Product Studio may display this only as an editorial concept record. It cannot use “bestseller,” “coming soon” urgency, preorder language, a price, book cover presented as final, invented excerpt, author testimonial, or delivery promise. A concept thumbnail must be labelled **Internal cover direction — not approved artwork** until the founder approves a rights-cleared final cover.

## Release gate

The book cannot advance to approved-for-sale until the full manuscript, editing, rights, final formats, support, policy, delivery, and separate commerce approval exist. Paperback and audiobook are independent later gates, not automatic extensions of an ebook.
