# Weekly Compass Journal — Concept Brief v0.1

> **Internal concept only. Not a completed journal and not approved for sale.**  
> **Registry ID:** `north.release-b.weekly-compass-journal`  
> **Current gate:** `concept`

## Intended outcome

Create a durable 52-week digital journal that helps an adult make a calm weekly reset, notice wins and lessons, reflect on energy, and complete a quarterly review. It should build on the free Weekly Compass Preview while remaining fully useful without the NORTH app.

## Concept boundary

| Field | Concept boundary |
|---|---|
| Intended audience | Adults who have validated a need for a structured, long-form weekly reflection practice. |
| Not for | Therapy, diagnosis, performance scoring, clinical intervention, or a promise of habit transformation. |
| Working deliverables | 52 weekly reset pages, wins/lessons sections, energy reflection, and quarterly review pages. |
| Proposed price | $27 internal planning only; never display in Phase 2A. |
| App relationship | Optional daily implementation support; journal must stand alone. |

## Development requirements

A full year of prompts must be original, coherent, and edited for repetition, clarity, and emotional safety. The product must have a final interior system, readable typography, fillable and printable QA, cover rights, a support plan, delivery plan, and final policy before it can be offered. The free Weekly Compass Preview remains the honest available starting point during development.

## Validation gate

The founder must validate that buyers want a longer journal after using the free preview. No “52-week journal,” price, preorder, delivery, waitlist, or customer-outcome claim may be published until the whole interior and release path are complete.
