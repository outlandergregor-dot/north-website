# Financial Clarity Kit — Concept Brief v0.1

> **Internal concept only. Not a completed kit, not financial advice, and not approved for sale.**  
> **Registry ID:** `north.release-b.financial-clarity-kit`  
> **Current gate:** `review_required`

## Intended outcome

Organize a household cash-flow conversation and recurring review in general educational terms only. The concept may help adults see categories and prepare questions for qualified professionals; it must never tell someone what to invest, borrow, repay, insure, save, or do with personal financial circumstances.

## Intended buyer and exclusions

| Field | Concept boundary |
|---|---|
| Intended audience | Adults seeking general money organization and household-conversation structure. |
| Not for | Individualized financial, investment, tax, credit, lending, insurance, legal, debt, or retirement advice. |
| Potential components | Cash-flow organizer, Safe Number worksheet, monthly review, household conversation sheets. |
| Product-to-app relationship | Optional organisation reinforcement only; no app entitlement or data connection. |
| Proposed price | $27 internal planning only; never display in Phase 2A. |

## Concept production requirements

The founder must provide original curriculum and examples that do not use real customer scenarios or imply outcomes. A qualified financial professional and legal reviewer must review the full curriculum, all disclaimers, all examples, marketing copy, age treatment, support escalation language, and delivery model before the status can progress beyond review-required.

## Required concept-to-production evidence

1. Founder-approved original curriculum and scope statement.
2. Qualified financial-professional review with documented reviewer scope.
3. Legal review of claims, disclaimers, policy, audience, and jurisdictional limitations.
4. Final asset, delivery, support, refund, and merchant process.
5. Human-reviewed locale path for every language eventually offered.

## No-sale rule

Until all evidence above is complete, the product is hidden. It may not display a price, checkout, preorder, waitlist, delivery claim, affiliate link, or request for personal financial data. This concept must never be marketed to minors.
