# NORTH 90-Day Direction Sprint — Concept Brief v0.1

> **Internal concept only. Not a completed workshop, program, workbook, or approved product.**
> **Registry ID:** `north.release-c.north-90-day-direction-sprint`
> **Current gate:** `concept`

## Intended outcome

Develop a guided 90-day direction experience that helps an adult choose one meaningful direction, establish a weekly rhythm, gather evidence of progress, and create a grounded next-cycle plan. The completed programme must stand alone, with the NORTH app as an optional implementation companion only.

## Concept boundary

| Field | Concept boundary |
|---|---|
| Intended audience | Adults ready for a fully developed longer direction programme. |
| Not for | Coaching substitution, therapy, performance guarantee, or an unbuilt curriculum marketed as a programme. |
| Working deliverables | Complete curriculum, Direction Book/workbook, a clear completion journey, and a support path. |
| Related later product | The NORTH 90-Day Direction Book follows only after the Sprint curriculum and buyer feedback validate the need. |
| Proposed price | Not set in Phase 2A. |

## Curriculum requirements

Before public product language exists, the founder must approve an original end-to-end curriculum: onboarding, direction selection, weekly rhythm, evidence/wins practice, review moments, completion experience, and next-cycle plan. Each lesson must name its intended action, time expectation, materials, constraints, and support boundary. The workbook requires its own original content, layout, accessibility, and print/digital QA.

## Evidence before progression

1. Founder-approved complete curriculum and workbook.
2. A real support and completion path.
3. Original-content and rights review.
4. A controlled pilot or buyer-feedback plan, approved separately.
5. Final delivery, support, refund, policy, and merchant process.
6. Human-reviewed full locale path for any released language.

## No-sale rule

This concept remains hidden in public contexts. No checkout, price, waitlist, urgency, transformation promise, customer count, testimonial, or delivery promise may appear until the entire programme is complete and a later commerce phase is approved.
