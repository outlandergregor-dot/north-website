## Commerce, privacy, and delivery controls

All commerce remains inactive. The founder decision materials define the future payment sequence as product page → merchant-hosted checkout → verified payment → confirmation/receipt → secure delivery → support/refund record. NORTH must not collect, transmit, or store payment-card data. No merchant provider has been selected or connected.

The future pilot model uses minimum-data measurement only after separate legal/privacy review and founder approval. It prohibits live tracking pixels, behavioral profiling, customer accounts, newsletter capture, automatic sharing, and sensitive NORTH-data collection in Phase 2F. The future opt-in sharing specification remains private-by-default and explicitly user-triggered.

## Localization and catalog governance

The 14 verified app locales, locale-aware route system, canonical/hreflang structure, and Arabic RTL foundation remain preserved. English is the only product-candidate source language. Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish (Latin America) remain the first future human-review queue; Arabic requires additional RTL product/print QA before any future release. No product edition is represented as available outside a fully human-reviewed product, checkout, delivery, support, and legal/policy stack.

The governed roadmap retains five connected product families: Financial Clarity, Routine Reset, Focus & Execution, Direction & Motivation, and Authority & Education. Every future product must reinforce the NORTH Method: Safe Number, Big Thing, 1-3-5 execution, weekly reflection, routines, recovery, and growth. Financial products remain general educational concepts only and require legal/policy review before any sale.
