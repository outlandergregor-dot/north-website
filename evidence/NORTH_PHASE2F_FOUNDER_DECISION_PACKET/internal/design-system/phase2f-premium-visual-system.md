# NORTH Premium Visual System — Phase 2F

> **Brand source:** Official NORTH compass icon supplied by the founder. The icon is retained as `assets/brand/north-compass-official-512.png`; generated favicon variants are derived directly from that source. No substitute logo has been introduced.

## Brand premise

NORTH should feel like a calm premium life operating system: composed rather than loud, directional rather than motivationally aggressive, and useful rather than performative. The visual system uses **midnight navy** for authority and depth, **warm ivory** for reflection and reading comfort, and **restrained compass gold** for direction, emphasis, and controlled celebration.

## Signature rules

The official compass icon appears only as an intentional brand mark: a favicon/app-icon variant, a small navigation identifier, a product cover seal, or a quiet section marker. It should not be stretched, recolored, placed on busy imagery, used as repeated decoration, or turned into generic clip art. Gold should lead the eye, not fill the page. Navy fields should hold one clear editorial message and enough negative space for it to breathe.

Typography should pair a quiet editorial serif for major statements and product titles with a disciplined sans-serif for navigation, interfaces, metadata, and forms. Sections need generous vertical rhythm, visible hierarchy, accessible color contrast, and an explicit focus state. Motion is limited to fast, non-essential opacity/position transitions and must respect reduced-motion settings.

## Product-cover system

Each future cover needs an icon/seal zone, title zone, subtitle/outcome zone, edition/version zone, and a private/public status marker where appropriate. The cover must state the actual format and edition; it must not imply a hardcover, shipped kit, course, app entitlement, or localized edition that does not exist.

For the 1-3-5 Execution Planner Core Digital Edition, the official compass icon acts as the small gold directional seal on a midnight-navy textured surface. The product should use the generated original product visuals only as founder-review assets until founder approval, rights confirmation, and final product-page QA.

## Accessibility and localization

The visual system must maintain text contrast independently of decorative imagery, provide meaningful image alt text, keep focus outlines visible on both navy and ivory fields, and work at 390px and high zoom without horizontal scroll. Arabic retains the same token and icon system but mirrors layout through true RTL controls; icon placement must be reviewed in-context rather than mirrored mechanically. Typography and long-product-title stress tests are required for every new locale.

## Review checklist

Before any product visual becomes public, the founder must confirm that the icon uses the official source, the product/edition/formats are truthful, no visual implies a result or unavailable benefit, images/illustrations are original or licensed, text is readable in print and screen contexts, and the locale / RTL treatment has been reviewed where relevant.
