# Phase 2F Visual QA Notes

## Premium homepage review

The homepage now uses the supplied official NORTH compass icon successfully in the header, footer, and hero-side method panel. The visual direction is stronger and more premium than the prior generic mark: the midnight navy hero has better depth, the restrained gold reads as a controlled accent rather than a bright marketing color, and the App Store / Free Tools actions remain the only visible public actions. The homepage still preserves the honest public boundary that Release A products are in founder review and not purchasable.

The brand application is coherent across the public shell. The small compass icon works well in the sticky header and footer, and the hero-side panel feels more aligned with the app icon. The hierarchy remains readable at desktop width, and the public-safe trust statement is still visible and credible.

## Planner print-proof review — US Letter candidate pages 1–8

The private release candidate cover and front-matter pages are readable, orderly, and visually premium. Page 1 communicates the private-review boundary clearly. Page 2 states the edition and founder-pilot candidate cleanly without implying customer availability. Page 3 introduces the customer-ready master with strong hierarchy and adequate breathing room.

Pages 4–8 confirm that the interior typography is readable and consistent. Section headings are clear, the gold divider treatment is restrained, writing lines are visible, and page-foot metadata remains present without dominating the page. The Two-Week Intention layout reads cleanly, and the Day 1 page has usable writing space and logical field order.

## Print observations to retain

The current print candidate is strong enough for founder review, but there is still a refinement opportunity: the early interior pages carry substantial white space, especially around the orientation and opening pages. This is not a blocker for private review, but it should be considered during future final production polish so the finished commercial edition feels intentionally paced rather than merely sparse.

No misleading commerce, delivery, or account implication was observed in the reviewed homepage or print-proof pages.
