## Visual decisions retained

The Phase 2B review environment uses a typography-led visual direction with deep navy, off-white paper, warm-gold status detail, and restrained blue-grey secondary copy. No placeholder product photography, app screenshots, final cover, stock imagery, testimonial, or false physical-product rendering is used. Final art direction remains a founder decision and a separate rights-review task.
