## Non-negotiable dependencies before any activation

1. Founder approval of the final English product master, asset selection, exact file bundle, audience, product page, and one pricing option.
2. Qualified legal review of seller identity, license, support/refund policy, consumer rights, privacy, tax / jurisdiction language, and customer communications.
3. Tagged / fillable PDF accessibility QA if fillable customer files will be offered.
4. Merchant-of-record selection, secure product configuration, pricing/tax/refund setup, and privacy review in a non-public environment.
5. End-to-end controlled test of checkout, receipt, confirmation, delivery, re-delivery, refund, support escalation, and access failure handling.
6. Human-reviewed product-content, checkout, delivery, support, and policy assets before any non-English product is claimed or offered. Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish (Latin America) remain first candidates; Arabic requires full RTL deliverable QA.
7. A new written founder approval that explicitly authorizes commerce activation. Nothing in Phase 2D is a substitute for that approval.
