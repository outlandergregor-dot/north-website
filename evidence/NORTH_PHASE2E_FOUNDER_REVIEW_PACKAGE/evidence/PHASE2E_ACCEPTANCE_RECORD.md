## Remaining activation gates

1. Founder approves the Core Digital product, final public-facing content, visual selection, price, and beta plan.
2. Qualified legal/policy review approves license, refund/support information, privacy, consumer disclosures, seller responsibility, and product claims.
3. Founder selects a merchant only after re-verifying current terms, seller eligibility, tax/receipt obligations, geography, data handling, delivery, refunds, and support operations.
4. Merchant, payment, receipt, signed-event verification, secure delivery, re-delivery, support, refund, and failure cases pass non-public end-to-end testing.
5. Any locale must complete human-reviewed product content, product page, PDF/print proof, policy, support/refund, checkout, and delivery work before an edition can be offered.
6. Commerce activation requires separate explicit written founder approval.
