## 1. The future licensed package

When activated in a future approved release, the **NORTH 1-3-5 Execution Planner** would consist of the English print-ready US Letter PDF, English print-ready A4 PDF, any expressly included editable or screen-fillable files, the start guide, and the fictional worked example (collectively, the **Planner Package**). The actual order confirmation and final product page must identify every included file before a purchaser completes any transaction.

## 2. License grant

Subject to payment in a future approved checkout and compliance with this license, NORTH would grant the original purchaser a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to download, store, print, and use the Planner Package for that purchaser’s own personal planning and reflection.

The purchaser may keep a reasonable number of backup copies and may print copies for the purchaser’s own use. The purchaser may not share a file, link, password, editable source, or printed reproduction with another person except where a future approved organizational license expressly permits it.

## 3. Restrictions

A purchaser may not sell, sublicense, rent, lease, redistribute, publish, post, upload, resell, bundle, translate, adapt for distribution, remove ownership notices from, train a model on for distribution, or claim authorship of any part of the Planner Package. The purchaser may not use the Planner Package as a white-label template, client deliverable, group-program handout, employer resource, or course material without a separate written license from the future contracting NORTH entity.

The purchaser may write personal answers in the planner and may discuss the method in their own words. Ownership of the underlying content, layout, prompts, visual assets, and brand elements remains with NORTH or its licensors.

## 4. Product boundary

The Planner Package is a self-guided organizational tool. It is not legal, medical, mental-health, financial, tax, investment, employment, or other professional advice. It is not a diagnosis, treatment, performance guarantee, coaching service, or promise of a particular outcome. A purchaser is responsible for deciding whether the tool is appropriate for their circumstances and for seeking qualified support where needed.

## 5. Availability and updates

The future product page must accurately state the file formats, supported languages, accessibility status, update policy, and support channel in effect at purchase. No purchaser should be promised translations, accessibility features, cloud storage, app access, coaching, or future updates unless those items are specifically included in the approved purchase terms.

## 6. Copyright and feedback

All rights not expressly granted are reserved. If a purchaser voluntarily sends feedback, they may do so without granting access to their private planner entries. Any future feedback policy must explain how feedback is used and must not claim rights over a purchaser’s personal content beyond what is necessary to respond to the feedback.

Before any sale, the approved version must name the exact legal seller, registered address or required contact information, governing law, consumer-rights language, tax treatment, dispute process, accessibility contact, privacy notice, effective date, and version number. Those items are deliberately absent from this founder-review draft because they are not yet confirmed.
